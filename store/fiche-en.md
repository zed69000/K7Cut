# BEAT SPLASH – English

**Description courte** (80 max) :
Cut, tap, scratch on the beat: every move plays the music and paints your canvas

**Description complète** :
Swipe, tap, hold the note, scratch the record: in BEAT SPLASH, you're the one playing the song, with K7, the mascot tape.

Tapes roll across the strip and every move landed right on the beat adds your part to the music: a brass hit, a crunchy guitar, a reggae offbeat. Miss a note and your part drops out. Chain them and the music swells into a trance, with the scenery going wild along with you.

🎵 10 TAPES, 10 STYLES
French house, pop funk, disco, hip-hop, roots reggae, orchestral, night synthwave, neo-soul, alternative rock and metal. Every side has its own composed score, its drums and a new move to learn.

✋ SIMPLE MOVES, A REAL CHALLENGE
Slice the brick the way its chevrons point: warm colors when the move goes up, cool colors when it goes down. Tap the circles, tap with two fingers, hold the long notes, scratch the record. A tutorial shows each new move at the right moment, on the real shape.

🎨 EVERY RUN PAINTS A CANVAS
Your rhythm becomes a painting: a clean dot when you're spot on, splashes when you're early, drips when you're late. Keep your best works and share them.

☠ THE CASSETTE OF DEATH
Three hearts, songs back to back without a break, faster and faster: hold on as long as you can and climb the world ranking.

👑 4 LEVELS, FROM APPRENTICE TO KING
Start gently, earn discs to open the next tapes, then replay the same side one level up.
