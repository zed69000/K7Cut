# BEAT SPLASH – English

**Description courte** (80 max) :
Cut, tap, scratch on the beat: every move plays the music and paints your canvas

**Description complète** :
Swipe, tap, hold the note, scratch the record: in BEAT SPLASH, you're the one playing the song.

Tapes roll across the screen and every move you nail right on the beat adds your part to the music: a brass stab, a fuzzed-out guitar, a reggae offbeat. Miss a note and your part drops out. Chain them together and the music builds and builds, all the way to trance.

🎵 10 TAPES, 10 STYLES
French house, pop funk, disco, reggae roots, boom bap hip-hop, orchestral, night synthwave, neo-soul, alternative rock, metal. Every side has its own composed score, its own drums and a brand-new move to learn.

✋ SIMPLE MOVES, A REAL CHALLENGE
Cut the block in the arrow's direction (green up, blue right, yellow diagonal), tap the round buttons, hold the long notes, scratch the record back and forth. A tutorial shows each new move at just the right moment, on the real shape.

🎨 EVERY RUN PAINTS A CANVAS
Your rhythm turns into a painting: a clean dot when you're spot on, splashes when you're early, drips when you're late. Keep your finest works.

👑 4 LEVELS, FROM APPRENTICE TO KING
Start nice and easy, earn discs to unlock the next tapes, then replay the same side one level up. At King, no mercy: three misses in a row and the tape snaps.
